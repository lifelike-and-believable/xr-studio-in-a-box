# Kai, performer

<!-- Stories link to a section, for example persona-performer#goals. -->

## Goals

- Feel the puppet respond at once, within 100 ms of moving.
- Stay in character whatever happens, and hear or feel a problem without breaking the performance.
- Drive a body, a face, a voice, several creatures, or a share of one character with other performers.
- Fire cues, conduct a crowd or hand over a channel while performing.
- Sense the audience without watching hundreds of avatars.
- Decide what may be captured and how it may be used, including any AI use, and change that decision later.
- Work within limits on time in a headset or suit, and stop whenever they need to.

## Skills and tools

- Physical craft in dance, circus, theatre or puppetry.
- Wears a portable inertial suit, a headset and hand controllers, or works with webcam or phone capture, or in an optical studio.
- Does not use code or the command line, and is not expected to use chat with an agent.

## Context of use

On a stage, in a capture volume or at home, wearing equipment and often in a headset, so cannot read a screen or use a keyboard. Performances may be improvised and live, where quick recovery matters more than polish. A solo performer may be running a public show with no operator, and needs emergency stop, pause and handover to work while performing.

## Frustrations

- A dropped device can leave the character frozen or broken in front of an audience.
- A stray gesture could fire a cue.
- Delay between moving and seeing the puppet breaks the sense of being the character.
- Long calibration before each session **[assumed]**.
- Uncertainty about what happens to recorded motion, face and voice after the show **[assumed]**.
