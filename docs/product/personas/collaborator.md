# Robin, collaborator with a say over the work

<!-- Stories link to a section, for example persona-collaborator#goals. -->

## Goals

- Agree terms for the project, including limits on AI use, cultural protocols and rights.
- Trust that those terms hold, and that the pipeline refuses work that breaks them.
- Review and sign off the specific items that need a decision, without learning the pipeline.
- See how the work uses what they contributed, and who else is credited.
- Change terms when a project changes, with a dated record.

## Skills and tools

- Expert in their own field, such as a cultural tradition, a body of work, or a partner company's business.
- Email, documents and video calls. Uses a link to a review page, not the production tools.
- May have no background in XR or generative AI, and may be wary of it.

## Context of use

Occasional and remote, usually fitting a review around other work. Sees only the items shared with them. Decisions can carry cultural or legal weight and may need discussion with others before an answer **[assumed]**. A funder given access for a report uses the same limited view.

## Frustrations

- Being asked to approve something without the context to judge it **[assumed]**.
- Not knowing where AI was used, or what happened to material they provided **[assumed]**.
- Terms agreed in a conversation that do not reach the people doing the work **[assumed]**.
