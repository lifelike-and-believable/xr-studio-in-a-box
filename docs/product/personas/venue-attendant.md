# Taylor, venue attendant

<!-- Stories link to a section, for example persona-venue-attendant#goals. -->

## Goals

- Start the experience and get each group of guests through it on time.
- Fit, clean and hand out headsets, and help guests who are uncomfortable or confused.
- Pause or stop for a guest who needs help.
- Tell in plain language whether the system is ready, and what to do if it is not.

## Skills and tools

- Guest care and venue procedures.
- Uses the show-time and device tools only, not the production tools.
- No technical background is assumed. Does not use code, the command line, or chat with an agent.

## Context of use

On a venue floor, standing, with guests waiting, repeating the same session many times a day **[assumed]**. Employed by the venue or partner, may not have met the production team, and may have had little training on this particular show. The venue network may have no internet. Technical help may be remote or absent.

## Frustrations

- Instructions that use production or engineering language **[assumed]**.
- A problem that needs a technician who is not there **[assumed]**.
- Guests who need help in a headset, with no quick way to see what they see **[assumed]**.
