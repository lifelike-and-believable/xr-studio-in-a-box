# Jamie, audience member

<!-- Stories link to a section, for example persona-audience-member#goals. -->

## Goals

- Feel present in the performance, and be seen as part of it.
- Join with a link or code, with no setup trouble, and rejoin as the same avatar after losing connection.
- React and have those reactions reach the performers.
- Feel safe and comfortable: personal space, mute and block, captions, a seated or low-motion option.
- Know what is captured about them, and say no.

## Skills and tools

- Any level of technical skill, and no assumed experience of VR.
- Uses whatever is to hand: a headset, a PC, a browser or a phone.
- Does not use the pipeline. Meets only the join page, the show and its avatar.

## Context of use

In a venue, on a headset, among other guests; or at home on a screen, as an interactive guest in the live world or as a stream viewer with seconds of delay. Attends once, for a short time, with no training and no patience for setup **[assumed]**. May be a minor where a show allows it. Connections and devices vary widely.

## Frustrations

- Joining steps that eat into the show **[assumed]**.
- Discomfort or motion sickness in a headset **[assumed]**.
- Feeling like a spectator rather than present in the world **[assumed]**.
- Harassment or unwanted contact from other guests' avatars **[assumed]**.

## Evidence

Named in the concept, which notes that every quality bar is set by what audiences see and feel. Audience avatars were part of most past shows (discovery section 6), and Maamawi and Gloaming show the in-venue and remote cases. No audience research is on record, so frustrations are **[assumed]**. In-venue and remote guests are one persona for now. Split them if stories need to treat them differently.
