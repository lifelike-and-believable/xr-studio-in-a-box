# Riley, character, environment or animation artist

<!-- Stories link to a section, for example persona-artist#goals. -->

## Goals

- Get from a brief to a strong first draft quickly, and spend time on what makes the work good.
- Give written feedback on a draft and see it revised, with earlier versions kept and comparable.
- Keep working in the chosen tools and bring the results back without agents redoing them.
- Know what is waiting for review, and what the reviewer said.
- See that credit and provenance record the work done by hand.

## Skills and tools

- Character, environment or animation craft in tools such as Blender, Character Creator and Unreal Engine.
- Generation tools such as Higgsfield and Tripo, used directly or through agents.
- Does not use code or the command line, and does not need to know how the agents work.

## Context of use

Works at a workstation, inside the art tool or the Unreal Editor panels, and often joins for part of a production. May be new to the pipeline and starts without training. Chat with the agent is optional. Looks at what needs doing next, makes it, and hands it to a gate.

## Frustrations

- Character design, meshing and rigging are among the slowest stages today.
- Generated output looks finished before it is, and fixing it can take longer than starting over **[assumed]**.
- Feedback arrives in scattered places and earlier versions are hard to find **[assumed]**.
- Concern that AI use replaces artists or hides their contribution **[assumed]**. The concept's non-goals rule out replacement, and the design must show this in practice.
