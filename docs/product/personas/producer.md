# Jordan, producer

<!-- Stories link to a section, for example persona-producer#goals. -->

## Goals

- Know where every stage and asset stands, who owns it, and what is blocked.
- Keep budget, actual cost and schedule in one view, including generation, cloud and per-show running costs.
- Stop spend from passing the project's limit without someone deciding that it should.
- Get funding applications, reports, contracts, invoices and call sheets drafted from one record of project facts, then check them and send them.
- Bring new people onto a production quickly, and give them the right role without technical help.
- Keep the records each funder, tax rule and contract requires.

## Skills and tools

- Budgets, schedules, contracts, funder rules and crew logistics.
- Spreadsheets, calendars, email, and accounting or e-signature tools on larger productions **[assumed]**.
- Not a pipeline engineer. Uses the web dashboard and may use chat with the agent.

## Context of use

Works mostly from the web dashboard, away from the Unreal Editor **[assumed]**. Pressure peaks at funder deadlines, before rehearsals and when touring. Teams form per production and people come and go, so this person is often setting up access and schedules for someone new. Handles identifiers such as bank details and tax numbers, which agents must never see.

## Frustrations

- Generated assets and cloud services create costs that are hard to see until the bill arrives **[assumed]**.
- Figures and descriptions are copied between applications, budgets and reports, and drift apart **[assumed]**.
- Credits, AI-use statements and proof of spend must be gathered by hand at the end of a project **[assumed]**.
- Availability changes for crew who only join for part of a production **[assumed]**.

## Evidence

Named as a team role in discovery section 15. Goals come from the approved workflow and business requirements. No producer has been interviewed, and discovery does not record who does this work now beyond the approver, so the frustrations are **[assumed]**. On a solo production this role is held by the director ([persona-director](director.md)).
