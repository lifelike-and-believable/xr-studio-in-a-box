# Drew, sound designer or composer

<!-- Stories link to a section, for example persona-sound-designer#goals. -->

## Goals

- Get from a brief to a first draft of voices, music, effects and ambience, and spend time on what makes the sound good.
- Place sound in the world, and hear it as a guest would on each target.
- Keep one show with a right-sounding mix for each output: headset, venue speakers, stream and browser.
- Adjust levels and mixes during rehearsal, and keep the result without waiting for a new build.
- See that credit, licences and provenance record what was composed, licensed or generated.

## Skills and tools

- Sound design, composition or mixing for live or interactive work **[assumed]**.
- Audio workstations, and show tools such as QLab and Ableton Live, which past shows used **[assumed]**.
- Unreal Engine audio, or audio middleware **[assumed]**.
- Does not use code or the command line **[assumed]**.

## Context of use

Works in an audio workstation with headphones or monitors, then in the venue during rehearsal, listening beside Alex at the operator's position. Joins for part of a production and may be hired per project **[assumed]**. On a small show, the role may be held by the director or the operator **[assumed]**.

## Frustrations

- Sound arrives last, when picture and schedule are already fixed **[assumed]**.
- A mix that works in headphones does not work on venue speakers or a stream **[assumed]**.
- A small change needs a technical artist and a rebuild, when rehearsal time is short **[assumed]**.
- Generated music and voice carry unclear licences and consent **[assumed]**.
