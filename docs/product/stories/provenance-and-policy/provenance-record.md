# Keep every asset's history

As Morgan, the director, I want to have every asset carry its history from the day it enters the project so that I can show where work came from and what was agreed.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every asset shows its source, tool and version, date, whether AI was used, who approved it and the terms in force.
- Prompts, settings, licence terms, later changes and reference inputs are kept with it.
- Prompts and reference inputs can be seen only by people whose role allows it.
- An asset with no record cannot enter a production build.
