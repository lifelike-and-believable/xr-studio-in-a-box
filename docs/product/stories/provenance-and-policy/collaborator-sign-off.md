# Sign off through a limited page

As Robin, a collaborator, I want to review and sign off an item through a limited page so that I can decide without learning the pipeline or seeing the rest of the project.

## Acceptance criteria

- Robin can open a page that shows only the item, the term it relates to and a plain statement of what is asked.
- Robin can sign off, decline or comment, with no deadline pressure and no AI recommendation shown.
- Robin gets an email with a link to the page when an item is waiting.
- The named stage waits until Robin decides. If Robin declines, the item goes back to the person who asked, with Robin's comment, and does not move on.
- The decision is recorded with who gave it and when.
- Robin cannot reach anything else in the project.
- The director or their delegate can share an item with Robin on the same page as a preview for comment only, for example a recorded take of a character Robin designed. A recording of a performer is shared only if that performer's agreement allows it (story-session-recording-record-agreement). The page says no decision is asked, nothing waits for Robin, and any comment goes to the person who shared it. An agent cannot share a preview.
