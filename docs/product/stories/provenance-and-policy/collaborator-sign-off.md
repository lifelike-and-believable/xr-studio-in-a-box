# Sign off through a limited page

As Robin, a collaborator, I want to review and sign off an item through a limited page so that I can decide without learning the pipeline or seeing the rest of the project.

## Acceptance criteria

- Robin can open a page that shows only the item, the term it relates to and a plain statement of what is asked.
- Robin can sign off, decline or comment, with no deadline pressure and no AI recommendation shown.
- The named stage waits until Robin decides.
- The decision is recorded with who gave it and when.
- Robin cannot reach anything else in the project.
