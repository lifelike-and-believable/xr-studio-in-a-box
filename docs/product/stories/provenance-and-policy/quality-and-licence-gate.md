# Hold generated work at the quality and licence gate

As Morgan, the director, I want to keep a generated asset out of the project until it passes its checks so that nothing reaches the work without a quality and licence decision.

## Acceptance criteria

- A generated asset stays out of the project until it passes.
- The checks are that the file opens, it is named correctly, its provenance and licence status are recorded, and a person has looked at it.
- A failed asset shows what failed.
