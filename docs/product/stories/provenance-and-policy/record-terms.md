# Record a collaborator's terms

As Robin, a collaborator, I want to have my terms recorded, with their history and any end date so that I can trust they hold, and change them when a project changes.

## Acceptance criteria

- A person with the right role can record Robin's terms, including limits on AI use, cultural protocols and rights. The terms, and each change, take effect only when Robin confirms them on their limited page, and the confirmation is recorded with the date, as a performer confirms a changed agreement (story-session-recording-change-agreement). Until then, the person who recorded them sees them as not confirmed. Robin's terms can name an alternate who may decide on Robin's behalf (story-provenance-and-policy-collaborator-sign-off). An agent cannot record or confirm terms.
- Robin can see the terms recorded for them, with a dated history of changes, on the same limited page used for sign-off, and nothing else in the project beyond their own records (agreement, terms, contracts, pay, credits). From that page, Robin can request a change to their terms in plain words. The request goes to the review queue of the holder of the contracts and licence terms approval (story-project-record-name-approvers), and Robin sees its state.
- A term can have an end date, including one set by a contract (story-contracts-and-payment-draft-and-track-contracts). The assets it covers are flagged a lead time before that date, which the project sets.
- After the end date, those assets cannot be used in new work or new shows. Past shows and work already delivered stand as they are.
- A show still running at the end date alerts a person, who decides.
