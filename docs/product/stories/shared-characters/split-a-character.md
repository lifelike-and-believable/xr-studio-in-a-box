# Share one character between several performers

As Kai, the performer, I want to drive a share of one character while other performers drive the rest so that several of us can play one puppet together.

## Acceptance criteria

- Two or more performers can each own different channels of one character, such as one on the body and another on the face and voice, with channels assigned as in story-live-puppet-assign-channels.
- A channel has one owner at a time. Trying to assign an owned channel shows who owns it.
- Each performer is shown what they need to perceive of the others who share the character, through more than sight alone. What that is gets settled in a rehearsal test with at least two real performers sharing a proxy character, and the bar is set with those performers before the story is accepted.
- Each performer's measured delay stays within the delay target (story-live-puppet-measure-delay).
- The split is saved, and the next session can reuse it.
