# Make a mesh and rig from an approved design

As Riley, the artist, I want a mesh and rig made from an approved design and sent to their gate so that I get from a brief to a strong first draft quickly and spend my time on what makes the work good.

## Acceptance criteria

- Riley can start a mesh and rig only from a design approved at the character design gate (story-character-path-design-drafts), without code. Tripo makes the mesh and rig. Its output licence is recorded before its first real use (story-character-path-design-drafts).
- Each request and its result go through the same policy gate, spend limit, adapter, provenance record and quality and licence gate as a design draft (story-character-path-design-drafts).
- Meshy and Rodin can be tried behind the same adapter (story-agent-work-tool-adapter), under the same licence rule. A result from a trial is marked as a trial in its provenance record.
- The result opens in the Unreal Editor as an engine-ready character, one that passes the asset checks for each target the project uses (story-asset-checks-check-against-budget), on the common skeleton or with an automatic retarget (story-character-path-common-skeleton). Riley can preview it moving with a recorded portable inertial suit take (story-session-recording-record-and-replay).
- Riley can give written feedback and get a revised mesh or rig as a new version (story-review-and-status-feedback-and-versions).
- Riley sends the mesh and rig to the mesh and rig gate. It waits for the director or their delegate (story-review-and-status-hold-gated-hand-off), and an agent cannot pass it.
