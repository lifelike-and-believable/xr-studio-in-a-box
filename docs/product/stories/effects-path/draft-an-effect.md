# Draft an effect from a brief

As Riley, the artist, I want to turn a brief and references into a first draft of an effect so that I get to a strong first draft quickly and spend my time on what makes the effect good.

## Acceptance criteria

- Riley gives a brief and references, and an agent drafts a Niagara effect or an animated material in the project. Riley can play the draft in the Unreal Editor on a proxy character.
- Any generated part, such as a texture or flipbook, goes through the policy gate (story-provenance-and-policy-block-generation), the spend limit (story-agent-work-spend-limit), the adapter (story-agent-work-tool-adapter), the provenance record (story-provenance-and-policy-provenance-record) and the quality and licence gate (story-provenance-and-policy-quality-and-licence-gate), then review.
- Riley can open the draft in Niagara and change any part by hand. The provenance record shows which parts the agent drafted and which Riley made.
- Before the story is accepted, Sam runs a trial on the first show's effects, such as those around a creature growing or changing shape. For each effect, Sam records which parts the agent drafted usefully and which Riley rebuilt by hand. The record is kept in the project.
