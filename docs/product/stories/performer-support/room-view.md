# See a live view of the room

As Kai, the performer, I want to see a live view of the room, or a silhouette or depth view, while performing so that I can sense the audience without watching hundreds of avatars.

## Acceptance criteria

- A show declares which return feeds it uses: a live room view, venue audio (story-performer-support-venue-audio) and the audience summary through physical signals (story-performer-support-audience-summary-signals).
- Kai can see a live view of the room, or a silhouette or depth view that shows shapes and movement but no faces, in a headset, or on a monitor placed where only the named cast and crew can see it, as the show sets. The test checks that the monitor cannot be seen from any guest area.
- The view is never recorded, and reaches no output. Only the cast and crew named for the session can open it, and nobody else can.
- The view cannot be turned on for a show with guests until its notice to guests is recorded. The notice's content is defined by REQ-governance-7 and REQ-performance-16: the view is live only, never recorded, and seen only by named cast and crew. A person who holds the audience-facing safety and consent approval approves the notice. An agent cannot approve it.
- Until the legal review of a live room view (REQ-governance-7) is recorded, the view is used only in tests with crew who know about it.
