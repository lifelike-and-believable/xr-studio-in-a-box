# Hear the venue while performing

As Kai, the performer, I want to hear venue audio while performing so that I can sense the audience without watching hundreds of avatars.

## Acceptance criteria

- Kai can hear venue audio in a headset or earpiece while performing, at a level Casey sets.
- The crew channel (story-performer-support-crew-channel) stays audible over the venue audio.
- Venue audio sent to Kai is never recorded, reaches no output, and is heard only by the cast and crew named for the session. A show uses it only with a notice to guests approved in the same way as the live room view's (story-performer-support-room-view).
- Until a legal review (REQ-governance-7) records that live venue audio carrying guests' voices may be used with a notice instead of each guest's consent, venue audio is used only in tests with crew who know about it.
- The delay from the room to Kai's ear is measured and shown.
- Venue audio works on the venue network with the internet off.
