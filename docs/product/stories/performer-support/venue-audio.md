# Hear the venue while performing

As Kai, the performer, I want to hear venue audio while performing so that I can sense the audience without watching hundreds of avatars.

## Acceptance criteria

- Kai can hear venue audio in a headset or earpiece while performing, at a level Casey sets.
- The crew channel (story-performer-support-crew-channel) stays audible over the venue audio.
- Venue audio sent to Kai is never recorded or transcribed, reaches no output, and is heard only by the cast and crew named for the session.
- Venue audio cannot be turned on for a show with guests until its notice to guests is recorded. The notice's content is defined by REQ-governance-7, and a person who holds the audience-facing safety and consent approval approves it, never an agent.
- Until the legal review of venue audio (REQ-governance-7) is recorded, venue audio is used only in tests with crew who know about it.
- The delay from the room to Kai's ear is measured and shown.
- Venue audio works on the venue network with the internet off.
