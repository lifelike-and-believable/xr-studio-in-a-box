# Develop the look from approved concept art

As Riley, the artist, I want to develop an environment's look from approved concept art, through written feedback so that I see it revised, with earlier versions kept and comparable.

## Acceptance criteria

- Riley can get environment concept art as images from Higgsfield from a brief and references, under the licence rule of story-character-path-design-drafts and through the same chain as a design draft. Riley sends one to the look and concept art gate, where it waits for the director or their delegate (story-review-and-status-hold-gated-hand-off). An agent cannot pass it.
- Riley can choose concept art that was accepted at the look and concept art gate, and ask an agent to bring the level's materials, lighting, sky and set dressing towards it.
- The look changes on the existing layout. After a look revision, the level is still walkable (story-environment-path-generate-a-world) and the layout accepted at the gate (story-environment-path-approve-at-the-gate) has not moved.
- Any generated material or texture goes through the same chain as other generated work, as listed in story-environment-path-generate-a-world.
- Riley can give written feedback and get a revised version, and compare two versions side by side with the concept art (story-review-and-status-feedback-and-versions).
- Riley can change any part of the look by hand in the Unreal Editor. A later agent revision keeps Riley's hand changes unless Riley asks for them to change.
