# Try a change from a link

As Morgan, the director, I want to open a link to a change to XR Studio and try it as the people on a made-up project so that I can decide whether it is right before a real production relies on it, with nothing to install.

## Acceptance criteria

- Each change to XR Studio that is waiting for Morgan's decision has a link. Opening it shows a trial copy of XR Studio with that change, in a browser, with nothing installed.
- The trial copy holds only made-up people, projects and files. A line on every page says so, and says not to enter real information.
- Morgan signs in as any of the made-up people with one selection, with no email and no password, and can act as two of them at once in two windows.
- Morgan chooses a tutorial and the trial copy is put at that tutorial's starting point, so Morgan does not have to do the earlier tutorials first.
- No email leaves a trial copy. Each message XR Studio would have sent can be read in the trial copy, so Morgan can check its wording and follow its link.
- Signing in as a made-up person is not possible in the XR Studio a production uses: the page for it does not exist there.
- A person on Morgan's team can start the same trial copy on their own computer with one command.
