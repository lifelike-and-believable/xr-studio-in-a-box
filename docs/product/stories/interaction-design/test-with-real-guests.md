# Check comfort and clarity with real guests

As Quinn, the interaction designer, I want to run a session where real guests try a design version, record what they say and see the changes it leads to so that I can check comfort and clarity with real people and see the changes that result.

## Acceptance criteria

- Quinn can plan a test session for one version of a design (story-interaction-design-record-design): the interactions to try, what to watch for, the questions to ask, the number of guests and the comfort checks. The director or their delegate approves the plan. Guests are told what is recorded about them in a notice approved by the holder of the audience-facing safety and consent approval (story-guest-care-notices-and-warnings), and comments are kept only as each guest agrees (story-guest-care-guest-data). A guest can stop at any time.
- During the session, Quinn or an observer they name records each comment and comfort problem against the interaction it concerns, with the time and the guest's session label, not their name.
- After the session, Quinn sees the comments grouped by interaction and marks each as a change, no change with a reason, or later. Each change makes a new design version that links back to the comments behind it, and goes through the interaction design gate. Only Quinn, the director or their delegate, and people whose role needs them can see the comments.
- An agent can group comments and draft change proposals, marked as drafted by an agent, but cannot mark a comment, accept a change or approve a version.
- The story is tested with simulated guests and comments, and is accepted after one session with real guests on the first show's design (UC-2).
