# Keep a personal space around each guest

As Jamie, the audience member, I want a personal space around my avatar, inside which other guests' avatars fade from my view, so that I feel safe and comfortable among other guests.

## Acceptance criteria

- Each guest's avatar in the world has a personal space. When another guest's avatar comes inside it, that avatar fades out of Jamie's view and Jamie's out of theirs, until it leaves. The size of the space and how avatars fade are part of the show's moderation rules (story-audience-voice-moderation-rules), set by the director or their delegate and approved by the holder of the audience-facing safety and consent approval. An agent cannot set or approve them.
- Jamie can make their own space larger, up to a limit the show sets, but not smaller than the show's size.
- The space works between guests in the venue and interactive guests alike. Guests who share a physical space always stay visible to each other inside personal space, as an outline, so nobody walks into a real person. Only the avatars of remote guests fade. How performers' characters come near guests is part of the interaction design (story-interaction-design-record-design).
- No position a personal space uses is stored or recorded. The space works with every agent and the internet disconnected for guests in the venue.
- The story is tested with simulated guests driven into each other's space, on the budget file's test device (story-asset-checks-check-against-budget), and once in a rehearsal with real guests in headsets.
