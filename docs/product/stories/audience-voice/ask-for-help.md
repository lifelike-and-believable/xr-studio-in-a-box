# Ask for help or report a guest

As Jamie, the audience member, I want to ask for help, or report another guest, in two steps from wherever I am in the show so that I feel safe and comfortable.

## Acceptance criteria

- In every tier (epic-remote-guests) and in the venue, Jamie can ask for help, or report a guest, in at most two steps from the show's menu. Jamie picks the guest by pointing at their avatar or from a list of nearby guests by display name. The menu works with the access options Jamie chose (story-guest-care-access-options).
- The request reaches the staff named for the session: in the venue, Taylor and Alex; online, Alex or the host named in story-remote-guests-host-online-audience. It shows Jamie's session label, the reported guest's session label, if any, and the time, in text and with a sound. Jamie is told in text that it was received, and again when someone responds.
- Staff respond with the existing controls: pausing one guest (story-session-operations-pause-one-guest), or muting, hiding or removing a guest (story-audience-voice-remove-a-guest). Jamie can also mute and block the guest at once (story-audience-voice-mute-and-block).
- A request holds only session labels and is used live only. It is kept only if staff record an incident (story-guest-care-record-an-incident). The reported guest is not told who reported them. A request never goes to an agent, and an agent cannot read or act on one. In the venue, requests work with every agent and the internet disconnected.
- The story is tested with simulated guests in each tier, and once in a rehearsal with real guests. The time from a request to staff seeing it is measured and shown against a target the director or their delegate sets.
