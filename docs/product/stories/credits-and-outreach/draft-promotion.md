# Draft promotional material, press kits and posts

As Morgan, the director, I want promotional material, press kits and posts drafted from the project's facts and approved assets so that I see drafts early, steer them and decide what is good before anything is published.

## Acceptance criteria

- An agent can draft promotional text, a press kit and posts from the project facts (story-project-record-record-facts), approved credits (story-credits-and-outreach-build-credits), an approved AI-use statement (story-credits-and-outreach-ai-use-statement) and approved assets. Each draft shows what it came from. Morgan can steer a draft with written feedback (story-review-and-status-feedback-and-versions).
- An asset, likeness, voice or recording is used in a draft only where its provenance record and the terms that cover it (story-provenance-and-policy-record-terms) allow promotional use, and a performer's likeness, voice or recordings only where their confirmed agreement includes use in promotion and press (story-session-recording-record-agreement, story-session-recording-change-agreement). Otherwise the draft leaves it out and says which term blocked it. No guest data is used.
- How the work is described to audiences, such as "immersive" or "VR", is settled by a test with real audiences that Jordan runs and records in the project facts before the first public campaign. Until it is recorded, drafts mark the wording as a choice for a person and no wording is assumed.
- Publishing, posting and sending to press wait at the publishing and marketing gate (story-review-and-status-hold-gated-hand-off), and only the holder of that approval (story-project-record-name-approvers) approves them. When material reaches that gate, the term and agreement check above runs again on what it holds, and anything no longer allowed is flagged, says which term blocked it, and blocks approval until it is removed. A person publishes or sends. An agent cannot (REQ-workflow-14).
- Approved material is versioned, and when a fact, credit or term behind it changes, it is flagged and says what changed.
- The story is tested with a sample project: a press kit is drafted, an asset whose terms forbid promotional use is checked to be left out, a recording of a performer whose confirmed agreement does not include use in promotion and press is checked to be left out, and an agent is checked to be refused when it tries to publish or send.
