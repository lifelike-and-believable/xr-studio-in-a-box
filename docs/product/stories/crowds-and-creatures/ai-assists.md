# Add secondary motion and fill undriven channels, with a switch for the performer

As Kai, the performer, I want AI to add secondary motion and fill the channels I am not driving, with a switch I control while performing, so that I decide whether AI is used with my performance and can change that decision at any time.

## Acceptance criteria

- Show-time AI is anything that chooses an action or pose the performer did not drive. In the first release it is rule-based and procedural and runs on the show machine with no generative model, no agent and no internet. Procedural motion that follows the performer's input deterministically, such as inverse kinematics or a body following the performer's position and intent (story-shared-characters-intent-and-procedural-motion), is not show-time AI: the AI switch does not turn it off and content limits do not check it. Crowds, creatures, AI secondary motion, AI-filled channels and AI-held poses all use it. A local generative model is a later trial behind the scripted fallback (story-behaviour-and-limits-scripted-fallback), not part of this story. AI secondary motion is motion added on top of the channels a performer drives, such as follow-through, overlap, breathing or a tail's swing, which never replaces a driven value. An AI-filled channel is a channel no source drives that a method choosing its own action drives from the performer's driven channels, cues and the scene, such as idle gestures on parts left unmatched (story-nearest-body-retargeting-match-nearest-body-plan). An AI-held pose is a fallback (story-handover-and-fallback-define-fallback) that holds the last good pose with rule-based idle motion, such as breathing and weight shift, and adds no new gesture. This story defines show-time AI, AI secondary motion, AI-filled channels and the AI-held pose. Other stories refer to them by this story's id.
- Sam, or another person with the technical artist's role, chooses in a mapping (story-shared-characters-map-a-different-body) which channels get AI secondary motion or are AI-filled, and Casey, or another person with the capture crew's role, may choose an AI-held pose as a fallback (story-handover-and-fallback-define-fallback). Each follows the character's behaviour specification (story-behaviour-and-limits-write-specification) and content limits (story-behaviour-and-limits-content-limits). AI assists use only live values and the character's rules, never anything learned from a performer's recordings. Continuing in a performer's style is later (REQ-characters-11).
- AI assists run on Kai's character only if Kai's confirmed agreement includes the AI assists use (story-session-recording-record-agreement). The performer's AI switch turns every AI assist on any character Kai drives off or on, including AI-filled channels no source drives. It starts each session in the state Kai last chose, and off until Kai first chooses. Kai uses it while performing, without a screen or keyboard, and it takes effect within the show's delay target (story-live-puppet-measure-delay). With AI off, secondary motion eases out with no visible jump (story-handover-and-fallback-hand-over-a-channel), AI-filled channels use their fallback, and a channel set to an AI-held pose uses a safe hold. Checked assist output stays within the show's delay target. Kai perceives the switch in at least two senses, with a pattern distinct from the performer signal (story-handover-and-fallback-performer-signal) and from Kai's cues. This story defines the performer's AI switch. Other stories refer to it by this story's id.
- Only Kai can switch AI back on for Kai's character, including assists switched off by a cue, an operator or a fallback (story-behaviour-and-limits-scripted-fallback). A person at the show whose role allows it, such as Alex, can switch AI off on any channel, and Kai is told in two senses. When an assist on Kai's character falls back for any reason, Kai is told in at least two senses, with a pattern distinct from the performer signal (story-handover-and-fallback-performer-signal). Nobody can disarm Kai's switch, by section or in any other way. Each use is logged with who used it. An agent cannot use the switch or change its state.
- A channel with a legibility bar (story-handover-and-fallback-legibility-bar) is never AI-filled and is never offered an AI-held pose. Its AI secondary motion is off while it is below its bar. If AI cannot run, a channel set to an AI-held pose uses a safe hold.
- The story is tested with recorded performer takes (story-session-recording-record-and-replay) on a proxy character, with the switch used during a take, with every agent and the internet disconnected, and on a channel with a legibility bar, then once with Kai performing live in rehearsal. Kai sets the bar for noticing the switch while performing before the story is accepted.
