# Record a performer's agreement

As Kai, the performer, I want my agreement for a project recorded, with any character brief I acknowledge, so that I decide what may be captured and how it may be used, including any AI use.

## Acceptance criteria

- The person who holds the contracts and licence terms approval records Kai's agreement for a project: which kinds of data may be captured (body, face, voice, motion, and sensor data from the body) and which uses are allowed. An agent cannot record or change it.
- The uses include driving a character live, standing in for Kai as a recording or fallback in a session or show, replay in rehearsal and tests, cleanup, and each AI use: training, synthesis, transformation, continuing a performance in Kai's style, driving a character after the performance, and AI assists on a character Kai drives (story-crowds-and-creatures-ai-assists).
- The agreement takes effect only when Kai confirms it, and the confirmation is recorded with the date. Until then, no capture source or sensor drives a character for Kai, and nothing of Kai's is recorded.
- Each performer has their own agreement in each project, so a different performer in the same role has a separate one.
- Where the policy record limits how a character may be portrayed, those limits are shown to Kai as a brief, and Kai's acknowledgement is recorded with the date. The pipeline does not filter Kai's live performance.
- Kai can see the agreement and brief recorded for them, with a dated history, on a limited page that shows nothing else in the project.
