# Change an agreement and flag what it no longer allows

As Kai, the performer, I want to have my agreement changed, and the assets it no longer allows flagged so that I can change my decision on what may be captured and how it may be used.

## Acceptance criteria

- A person with the right role records a change to Kai's agreement (story-session-recording-record-agreement), kept with a dated history. An agent cannot. Kai sees the change on their limited page.
- The project lists and flags the recordings and derived assets that the changed agreement no longer allows.
- Flagged recordings and assets cannot be used in new work or new shows, including replay in rehearsal and tests. Past shows and work already delivered stand as they are.
- A contract can set different terms. A person with the right role records them for Kai, and the project shows which terms apply and who recorded them.
- The default in the two criteria above is confirmed by legal review (REQ-governance-9) before this story is accepted. Until then, a changed agreement blocks every new use of the affected recordings and assets.
