# Accept, revise or reject work

As Morgan, the director, I want to accept, request revision or reject items, singly or in a batch so that I can approve quickly and keep the bar high.

## Acceptance criteria

- Morgan can accept, request revision or reject one item, or several at once.
- A revision request needs written feedback, and the requester sees it.
- A decision leaves the queue at once and is recorded with who made it and when.
