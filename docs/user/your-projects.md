# Your projects

After you sign in, you see **What needs you**: what is waiting for you on all your projects (see [What needs you](what-needs-you.md)). If you are not on any project yet, you see **Projects** instead. **Projects**, in the menu at the top of every page, lists every project you are on. Selecting **XR Studio** at the top of any page brings you back to **What needs you**.

## Find a project

On **Projects**, each project shows its name, its organisation, **Your roles** on it, and an **Owner** tag if you own it. Select a project to open it.

![Projects](../design/dashboard-shell/home-projects.png)

If you are not on any projects yet, you see "You are not on any projects." If you were invited to a project, open the link in your invitation email instead, or ask its producer or director to add you. If you are setting up your own production, select **Start a project** (you do not need to create an organisation first) or **Create an organisation** (see [Organisations and projects](organisations-and-projects.md)).

## Move between projects

Inside a project, the header has a button **Project: <project name>**.

1. Select it. Your projects are listed, grouped by organisation.
2. Select another project, or **All projects** to go back to **Projects**.

The header also has **What needs you**, **Projects** and **Organisations**, which take you to those pages.

## Your account and signing out

Select your name at the top of any page. The menu has **Your account**, where you add passkeys (see [Signing in](sign-in.md)), and **Sign out**.

## What you see depends on your roles

You see only the projects you are on, and inside each project only what your roles there allow. If someone changes your roles, the change shows the next time you do anything; you do not need to sign out. Show machines get role changes at their next show-package refresh, in a later release.

## If something goes wrong

| You see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **This page is not available** | The page does not exist, or none of your roles lets you see it. | If someone sent you the link, ask them to check you are on the project. Select **Go to your projects**. |
| "You no longer have access to this project" | Your roles on this project were changed or removed while you were using it. | Select **Go to your projects**. Ask the project's producer or director if you think this is a mistake. |
| "Nothing in the dashboard for you yet" | Your roles (for example Performer) do not use the dashboard. | Nothing to do. You will get an email with a link to your own page when there is something for you. If you need a different role, contact the person who added you. |
