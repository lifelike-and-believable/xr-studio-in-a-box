# Signing in and your account

This area covers getting into XR Studio and leaving it. Everyone who has a role that uses the dashboard needs it: Jordan, the producer, Morgan, the director, Riley, the artist, Sam, the technical artist, and Drew, the sound designer.

## The ideas you need

- XR Studio has no passwords. You sign in with a link sent to your email address, or with a passkey on a device you have used before.
- You cannot sign up. You can sign in once someone has added you to a project with a role that uses the dashboard. Performers, collaborators and venue attendants do not sign in to the dashboard.
- A link works once and for a limited time: 7 days for an invitation link, 15 minutes for a sign-in link.

## Pages

In the order a user needs them:

- [Signing in](sign-in.md): accept an invitation, sign in with an email link or a passkey, add a passkey on your account page, and sign out.
