# Review

This area is a project's Review tab. People who make work, such as Riley, the artist, and Drew, the sound designer, send it for review there. The people who approve a stage, such as Morgan, the director, and Sam, the technical artist, as a delegate, decide it there.

## The ideas you need

- Work at a gated stage waits until a person accepts it, asks for a revision, or rejects it. Nothing at a gate moves on without a person's decision.
- An AI can recommend a result, but it can never pass a gate.
- Who decides each stage is set on the project's Approvers tab: the approver or delegate of the stage's area, or the holder of its protected approval.
- A decision is final. It is recorded with the decider's name and the time, and cannot be changed afterwards.

## Pages

In the order a user needs them:

- [Review](review.md): send work for review, see and withdraw what you sent, see everything waiting, decide one item or several, and hand an item to your delegate.
