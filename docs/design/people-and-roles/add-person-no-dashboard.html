Roles

Choose one or more, listed in this order everywhere. A person can see and do what any of their roles allows. No role gives an approval: approvers are named separately.

Leads the creative work. In the dashboard today: sees the whole project, adds and removes people, and edits the project name and brief.
Runs the production. In the dashboard today: sees the whole project, adds and removes people, and edits the project name and brief.
Builds and checks the pipeline. In the dashboard today: sees the project and who is on it. Pipeline tools come in later releases.
Makes characters, environments and effects. In the dashboard today: sees the project and who is on it. Tools for making work come in later releases.
Makes the sound and music. In the dashboard today: sees the project and who is on it. Sound tools come in later releases.
Designs how guests take part. In the dashboard today: sees the project and who is on it. Design tools come in later releases.
Sets up and runs capture suits and sensors. In the dashboard today: sees the project and who is on it. Capture tools come in later releases.
Runs the show. In the dashboard today: sees the project and who is on it. Show tools come in later releases.
Looks after guests at a venue. In the dashboard today: nothing; no dashboard sign-in. They get show-time tools in a later release.
Performs live. In the dashboard today: nothing; no dashboard sign-in. They get a link to their own agreement page when there is something to confirm.
Contributes work under agreed terms. In the dashboard today: nothing; no dashboard sign-in. They get a link to a limited page for their own items when there is something to sign off.
