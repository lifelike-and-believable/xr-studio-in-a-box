Harbourfront Puppet Co.

Identifier formats

Text matching a format that is On is not saved where agents can read it, in any of Harbourfront Puppet Co.'s projects: facts and documents now, chat from a later release. A person can still save it by confirming it is not an identifier; each confirmation is recorded in Activity. Matching ignores spaces, hyphens and dots between characters, so 046 454 286 and 046-454-286 both match. People who look after Harbourfront Puppet Co. can change this list, and every change is recorded in its activity.
