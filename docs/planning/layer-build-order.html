Three things the graph shows

Layer 2 does not wait for Layer 3

Live puppetry is built and tested on proxy characters on the UE5 mannequin skeleton. Its only need from Layer 1 is the terms, policy gate and provenance behind the performer's agreement.

Layer 7 does not wait for Layers 5 or 6

Behaviour limits and crowds need the cue list, session operations, live signals and the character path. They can run beside delivery and the audience work.

Layer 3 is mostly off the critical path

Only three of its epics hold others up: asset-checks (builds), character-path (avatars, crowds) and interaction-design (branching shows).

Blockers outside the build

  • The legal review of REQ-governance-7 is pending. It gates guest-care, guest presence sensing and the performer's live room view.
  • The real optical capture test needs studio access. Until then it runs on a recorded or simulated stream.
  • Layer 2 needs proxy characters: the mannequin, a quadruped, a creature with extra limbs, and one outside the known set.

Limits of this analysis

  • It works at epic level. The 266 stories were not traced one by one.
  • Layers 2 to 7 have no designs yet, so they have no slice order. Only Layer 1 has one.
  • Stage boundaries are my reading of the dependencies, not an approved plan.
