# Clear your review queue

For **Morgan, the director**.

You open the work waiting for your decision, hand one item to your delegate, ask for a revision, reject one, and accept two at once. Then Riley, who sent them, reads your decisions.

## Before you start

- Morgan Ray is the director of The Lantern Keeper and decides Creative, with Sam Okafor, a technical artist, as delegate.
- Riley Chen, an artist, has sent six items for review: [Send work for review](send-work-for-review.md).

## Steps

1. Sign in as **Morgan**. Click **Projects**. Click **The Lantern Keeper**. Click **Review**. You see **5 items waiting for your decision, 1 blocking.**
2. Click **Harbour at night, lighting pass**. Click **Hand to Sam Okafor**. You see **Handed to Sam Okafor: Harbour at night, lighting pass.**
3. As **Sam**: Sign in as **Sam**. Click **Projects**. Click **The Lantern Keeper**. Click **Review**. Choose `Handed to you` from **Handed to**. Click **Apply filters**. Click **Harbour at night, lighting pass**. Type `Good. Keep the fog at this height.` into **Feedback for Riley Chen (needed to request a revision)**. Click **Accept**. You see **Accepted: Harbour at night, lighting pass.**
4. Click **Review**. Click **Lantern Keeper character sheet**. Type `Shorten the coat: it will catch on the lighthouse stairs.` into **Feedback for Riley Chen (needed to request a revision)**. Click **Request revision**. You see **Revision requested: Lantern Keeper character sheet.**
5. Click **Review**. Click **Lighthouse concept**. Click **Reject**. Type `Use the tower from the brief.` into **Feedback (optional)**. Click **Reject 1 item**. You see **Rejected: Lighthouse concept.**
6. Click **Review**. Click **Accept all 2 in Sound and music**. Click **Accept 2 items**. You see **Accepted 2 items in Sound and music.**
7. As **Riley**: Sign in as **Riley**. Click **Projects**. Click **The Lantern Keeper**. Click **Review**. Click **Sent by you**. You see **Accepted by Sam Okafor**, **Revision requested by Morgan Ray** and **Rejected by Morgan Ray**.

## What you made

A clear queue: Sam accepted the lighting, Riley has a revision to make on the character sheet, the concept is rejected, and both bell sounds are accepted. The licence still waits for Jordan, who holds Contracts and licence terms. Next: Riley sends the revised sheet in [Give feedback and compare versions](give-feedback-and-compare-versions.md).
