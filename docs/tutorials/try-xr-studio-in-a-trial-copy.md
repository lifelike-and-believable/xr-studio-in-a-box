# Try XR Studio in a trial copy

For **Morgan, the director**.

You open a trial copy, put it at a tutorial's starting point, and act as two of its made-up people: Jordan, the producer, who starts a project and adds Riley, and Riley, the artist, who signs in with the invitation Jordan's step sends. On the way you read the email the trial copy kept instead of sending.

## Before you start

- A trial copy of XR Studio. Open it from the link under "Try it" in the description of the change you were asked to decide on, and sign in to Vercel if you are asked to.
- A browser that can open a private window, to be Riley while you are Jordan.
- Jordan Lee is the producer of the made-up project. Riley Chen is an artist on it. Neither needs an email address or a password.

## Steps

1. As **Jordan**: Open `/try`. You see **Try XR Studio**, **Choose a tutorial** and **Sign in as a person**.
2. As **Jordan**: Click **Start: Try XR Studio in a trial copy**. You see **Start Try XR Studio in a trial copy?**.
3. As **Jordan**: Click **Start**. You see **This copy now holds the starting point for Try XR Studio in a trial copy** and **Not added yet. Jordan adds Riley in the tutorial.**
4. As **Jordan**: Click **Sign in as Jordan**. You see **Projects**.
5. As **Jordan**: Open `/projects/new`. Type `The Lantern Keeper` into **Project name**. Click **Start project**. You see **The Lantern Keeper**.
6. As **Jordan**: Click **People**. Click **Add a person**. Type `riley@xr.test` into **Email address**. Type `Riley Chen` into **Name (optional)**. Check **Artist**. Click **Add person**. You see **Riley Chen**.
7. As **Jordan**: Open `/try`. Click **Email sent by this copy**. You see **riley@xr.test**. Click **Open**. You see **Back to email sent by this copy**.
8. As **Riley**: Open `/try` in a private window. Click **Sign in as Riley**. You see **What needs you**.

## What you made

A trial copy that holds a project, The Lantern Keeper, with Jordan as its producer and Riley as an artist who has accepted the invitation. To try another tutorial, open **Try XR Studio** and select **Start** beside it. To learn the task itself in the product your production uses, read [Trying XR Studio](../user/help/trying-xr-studio.md).
