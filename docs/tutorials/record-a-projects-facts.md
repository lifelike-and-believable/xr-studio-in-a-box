# Record a project's facts

For **Jordan, the producer**.

You record The Lantern Keeper's description, budget and capacity once, change and restore a fact, add a work sample, and see what an artist sees.

<!-- runner: manual, because step 7 chooses a file, which the tutorial runner cannot do yet. -->

## Before you start

- Jordan Lee is the producer of The Lantern Keeper. Directors and producers change a project's facts.
- Riley Chen is an artist on the project.
- The file `lantern-test.png`, from **Files for the tutorials** on Try XR Studio in a trial copy, or any image of your own in your production.

## Steps

1. Sign in as **Jordan**. Click **Projects**. Click **The Lantern Keeper**. Click **Facts**. You see **Description**, **Plan**, **Budget** and **Capacity**, each **Not recorded yet.**
2. Click **Add description**. Type `A lighthouse keeper's last night, told with puppets and light.` into **Description**. Click **Save description**. You see **Description saved.**
3. Click **Add budget**. Type `148000` into **Total in Canadian dollars**. Click **Save budget**. You see **Total: $148,000 CAD.**
4. Click **Add capacity**. Type `forty` into the guests in the room, and `200` into the viewers online. Click **Save capacity**. You see "Enter the number of guests in the room as a whole number, like 40." Change `forty` to `40` and save again. You see **40 guests in the room and 200 viewers online, per show.**
5. Click **Edit description**, change the text, and save. Click **Earlier versions** beside Description. Click **Compare with current** beside version 1: added words are underlined and removed ones struck through.
6. Click **Restore version 1**, then **Restore version 1** in the dialog. You see **Description restored to version 1, saved as version 3.**
7. Click **Add work samples**. Type `Lantern test` into **Title**, choose the image `lantern-test.png`, and click **Add work sample**. You see **Lantern test added to the work samples.**
8. Click **Remove** beside Lantern test, then **Remove Lantern test** in the dialog. Click **Earlier versions**, then **Restore** beside version 1, and **Restore version 1**. Lantern test is back.
9. As **Riley**: Sign in as **Riley**. Open The Lantern Keeper's **Facts**. Riley sees the facts without **Edit** or **Add**, and **The budget is visible to the director and producer.** in place of the budget.

## What you made

The Lantern Keeper's description, budget, capacity and a work sample, each with its earlier versions kept. Next: [Keep bank details and tax numbers out](keep-bank-details-and-tax-numbers-out.md).
