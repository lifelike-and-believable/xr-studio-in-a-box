# Name who approves what

For **Jordan, the producer**.

You name who decides each area of The Lantern Keeper and who holds a protected approval, and see why a delegate cannot simply be removed.

## Before you start

- Jordan Lee is the producer of The Lantern Keeper. Only a director or producer can change approvers.
- Morgan Ray is the director, and Sam Okafor a technical artist, on the project.

## Steps

1. Sign in as **Jordan**. Click **Projects**. Click **The Lantern Keeper**. Click **Approvers**. You see **Areas** and **Protected approvals**.
2. Click **Change Creative**. Choose `Morgan Ray (invited, not signed in yet)` from **Approver**. Choose `Sam Okafor (invited, not signed in yet)` from **Delegate (optional)**. Click **Save Creative**. You see **Creative saved: Morgan Ray approves it, with Sam Okafor as delegate.**
3. Click **Change Performance and behaviour**. Choose `Morgan Ray (invited, not signed in yet)` from **Approver**. Choose `Morgan Ray (invited, not signed in yet)` from **Delegate (optional)**. Click **Save Performance and behaviour**. You see **Choose someone other than the approver as delegate, or No delegate.**
4. Choose `No delegate` from **Delegate (optional)**. Click **Save Performance and behaviour**. You see **Performance and behaviour saved: Morgan Ray approves it, with no delegate.**
5. Click **Change Publishing and marketing**. Choose `Morgan Ray (invited, not signed in yet)` from **Holder**. Click **Save publishing and marketing**. You see **Publishing and marketing saved: Morgan Ray holds it.**
6. Click **People**. Click **Change roles or remove Sam Okafor**. Click **Remove Sam Okafor from the project**. You see **Sam Okafor cannot be removed yet**.

## What you made

Morgan Ray decides Creative, with Sam Okafor as delegate, and Performance and behaviour alone, and holds Publishing and marketing. Sam stays on the project until Creative has another delegate. Next: Riley sends work for review in [Send work for review](send-work-for-review.md).
