# Keep your organisation's facts

For **Jordan, the producer**.

You record your company's bio and a funder applicant profile once, on the organisation's page, and see the project read them.

## Before you start

- Jordan Lee is the producer of The Lantern Keeper, a project in Harbourfront Puppet Co., and looks after the organisation. Only people who look after an organisation can change its facts.

## Steps

1. Sign in as **Jordan**. You see **What needs you**.
2. Click **Organisations**. Click **Harbourfront Puppet Co.**. You see **Organisation facts** and **Funder applicant profiles**.
3. Click **Add bio**. Type `A puppet company on the harbour front, making shows for small rooms since 2009.` into **Bio**. Click **Save bio**. You see **Bio saved. Every project in Harbourfront Puppet Co. now reads the new bio.**
4. Click **Add the profile for Canada Council for the Arts**. Type `Harbourfront Puppet Co.` into **Applicant name as registered with the funder**. Type `Arts organisation` into **Applicant category**. Type `Puppet theatre for audiences of all ages.` into **Mandate**. Click **Save profile**. You see **Applicant profile for Canada Council for the Arts saved.**
5. Click **Projects**. Click **The Lantern Keeper**. Click **Facts**. You see **From Harbourfront Puppet Co.** and **A puppet company on the harbour front**.

## What you made

Harbourfront Puppet Co.'s bio and its applicant profile for the Canada Council for the Arts, recorded once and read by The Lantern Keeper and every project you start in the organisation. Next: [Keep bank details and tax numbers out](keep-bank-details-and-tax-numbers-out.md).
