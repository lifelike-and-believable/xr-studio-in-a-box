# See where everything stands

For **Jordan, the producer**.

You open the board, give people their first work, see which work is blocked, and change an owner.

<!-- runner: manual, because step 4 chooses a file, which the tutorial runner cannot do yet. -->

## Before you start

- Jordan Lee is the producer of The Lantern Keeper. Directors and producers add work and choose its owner.
- Riley Chen is an artist, Sam Okafor a technical artist, Drew Patel a sound designer, and Morgan Ray the director, who decides Creative.
- The file `character-sheet.png`, from **Files for the tutorials** on Try XR Studio in a trial copy.

## Steps

1. Sign in as **Jordan**. Click **Projects**. Click **The Lantern Keeper**. Click **Board**. You see **Nothing is on the board yet.** Each stage says who decides it.
2. Click **Add work**. Type `Harbour ambience` into **Title**. Choose `Sound and music` from **Stage**. Choose Drew Patel as **Owner**. Click **Add work**. You see **Added Harbour ambience to Sound and music, owned by Drew Patel.**
3. As **Riley**: Sign in as **Riley**. Open The Lantern Keeper's **Review** and click **Send for review**.
4. As **Riley**: Send `Lantern Keeper character sheet` at **Character design**: choose the image `character-sheet.png` and choose `Mesh and rig` under **Is other work waiting on this decision?**.
5. Add the work `Lantern Keeper mesh and rig` at **Mesh and rig**, owned by Sam Okafor. Open the **Board**: the rig is **Blocked**, waiting on the character sheet, which is **Waiting for review** and **Blocking**. Click **Blocked work only**: only the rig is shown.
6. As **Morgan**: Sign in as **Morgan**. Open the **Board**. Morgan sees the same work, with the character sheet waiting for Morgan's decision.
7. Add the work `Harbour children character sheet` at **Character design**, owned by Sam Okafor. On its page, choose Riley Chen under **Owner** and click **Change owner**. Then click **Remove Harbour children character sheet**, and confirm. You see **Removed Harbour children character sheet from the board.**

## What you made

A board with Drew's first task, the character sheet waiting for Morgan, and the rig blocked behind it. Next: Drew finds the task in [Find what needs you](find-what-needs-you.md).
