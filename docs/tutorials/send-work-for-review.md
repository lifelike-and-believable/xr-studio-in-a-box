# Send work for review

For **Riley, the artist**.

You send your work for review at the stage it is ready for, say which other work waits on it, and see who decides it. Then Jordan sees everything waiting.

<!-- runner: manual, because each send chooses a file, which the tutorial runner cannot do yet. -->

## Before you start

- Riley Chen is an artist on The Lantern Keeper. Morgan Ray, the director, decides Creative, with Sam Okafor, a technical artist, as delegate: [Name who approves what](name-who-approves-what.md).
- Jordan Lee is the producer and holds Contracts and licence terms.
- The files `character-sheet.png`, `harbour-lighting.png`, `lighthouse-concept.png`, `bell-sounds.png` and `harbour-recordings-licence.pdf`, from **Files for the tutorials** on Try XR Studio in a trial copy.

## Steps

1. Sign in as **Riley**. Click **Projects**. Click **The Lantern Keeper**. Click **Review**. Click **Send for review**.
2. Type `Lantern Keeper character sheet` into **Title**. Choose `Character design` from **Stage**: a line says Morgan Ray or Sam Okafor decides it. Type `Front and side views.` into **What changed**. Choose the image `character-sheet.png`. Choose `Mesh and rig` from **Is other work waiting on this decision?**. Click **Send for review**. You see **Sent for review: Lantern Keeper character sheet. It waits for Morgan Ray or Sam Okafor.**
3. Send `Harbour at night, lighting pass` at **Environments**: choose the image `harbour-lighting.png`.
4. Send `Lighthouse concept` at **Look and concept art**: choose the image `lighthouse-concept.png`.
5. Send `Bell sounds, take 1` and `Bell sounds, take 2` at **Sound and music**: choose the image `bell-sounds.png` for each.
6. Send `Licence for the harbour recordings` at **Contracts and licence terms**: choose the PDF `harbour-recordings-licence.pdf`. It waits for Jordan Lee, the holder.
7. As **Jordan**: Sign in as **Jordan**. Open The Lantern Keeper's **Review**, then **Everything waiting**. The licence is listed with **You** under **Waiting for**.

## What you made

Six items waiting for review: five for Morgan or Sam, the character sheet blocking the mesh and rig, and the licence for Jordan. Next: Morgan decides them in [Clear your review queue](clear-your-review-queue.md).
