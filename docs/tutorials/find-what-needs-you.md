# Find what needs you

For **Drew, the sound designer**.

You sign in and find your first task, what to do with it, and who decides it. Then Morgan, the director, finds the work waiting for a decision and decides it from the same page.

## Before you start

- Drew Patel is a sound designer on The Lantern Keeper. Jordan Lee, the producer, has given Drew a first piece of work: [See where everything stands](see-where-everything-stands.md).
- Morgan Ray is the director and decides Creative work, where sound and music is.

## Steps

1. Sign in as **Drew**. You see **What needs you**, **1 thing needs you: 1 assigned to you.** and **How work moves on The Lantern Keeper**.
2. Click **Harbour ambience**. You see **Make it, then send it for review.**
3. As **Morgan**: Sign in as **Morgan**. You see **For your decision** and **Mesh and rig waits on this decision.**
4. As **Morgan**: Click **Lantern Keeper character sheet**. Type `Shorten the coat, as Sam says.` into **Feedback for Riley Chen (needed to request a revision)**. Click **Request revision**. You see **Revision requested: Lantern Keeper character sheet.**

## What you made

Drew knows the first task and who decides it, and Morgan has decided the work that was waiting, without opening the board. Next: [Give feedback and compare versions](give-feedback-and-compare-versions.md).
