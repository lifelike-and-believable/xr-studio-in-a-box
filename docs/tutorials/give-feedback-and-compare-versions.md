# Give feedback and compare versions

For **Riley, the artist**.

You read feedback on your work, send a revised version, compare two versions side by side, and restore the earlier one. Sam gives feedback and Morgan decides.

<!-- runner: manual, because step 3 chooses a file, which the tutorial runner cannot do yet. -->

## Before you start

- Riley Chen is an artist on The Lantern Keeper and has sent the character sheet for review: [See where everything stands](see-where-everything-stands.md).
- Sam Okafor is a technical artist and the Creative delegate. Morgan Ray is the director and decides Creative.
- The file `character-sheet.png`, from **Files for the tutorials** on Try XR Studio in a trial copy.

## Steps

1. As **Sam**: Sign in as **Sam**. Open the **Board** and click **Lantern Keeper character sheet**. Type `The long coat will catch on the lighthouse stairs.` into **Add feedback on version 1**. Click **Add feedback**. You see **Feedback added.**
2. As **Morgan**: Sign in as **Morgan**. Open the sheet from **What needs you**. Type `Shorten the coat, as Sam says.` as feedback for Riley Chen and click **Request revision**.
3. As **Riley**: Sign in as **Riley**. Under **Returned to you**, open the sheet and click **Send a revised version**. Type `Coat shortened to the knee.` into **What changed**, choose the image `character-sheet.png`, and click **Send for review**. You see **Sent for review: Lantern Keeper character sheet, version 2.**
4. As **Morgan**: Open the sheet. Type `The long coat read better from the back rows. Go back to version 1.` and click **Request revision**.
5. As **Riley**: Open the sheet and click **Compare versions**: version 1 and version 2 are side by side. Under version 1, click **Restore version 1**, then **Restore version 1** in the dialog. You see **Restored version 1 as version 3.**
6. As **Morgan**: Open the sheet and click **Accept**. On the **Board**, the sheet is **Done**.

## What you made

The character sheet at version 3, restored from version 1 and accepted, with every version and its feedback kept. Next: Jordan reads it all in [Read who changed what](read-who-changed-what.md).
