# Start a production and bring in your team

For **Jordan, the producer**.

You add a passkey and sign in with it, create your company's organisation, start a project in it, and add the four people who make it with you.

## Before you start

- An XR Studio account that can start a project: on a new installation, the first person can.
- A browser with a passkey provider: your device or a password manager.
- Jordan Lee is the producer. Jordan adds Morgan Ray, the director; Riley Chen, an artist; Sam Okafor, a technical artist; and Drew Patel, a sound designer.

## Steps

1. Sign in as **Jordan**. You see **Projects**.
2. Select **Jordan Lee**, then **Your account**. Under **Passkeys**, select **Add a passkey** and confirm in your browser or password manager. The passkey is listed with the device it was made on and today's date. (manual)
3. Select **Jordan Lee**, then **Sign out**. Select **Sign in again**, then **Sign in with a passkey**, and confirm. You see **Projects**. (manual)
4. Open `/organisations/new`. Type `Harbourfront Puppet Co.` into **Organisation name**. Click **Create organisation**. You see **Looked after by**.
5. Click **Start a project in this organisation**. Type `The Lantern Keeper` into **Project name**. Type `A lighthouse keeper's last night, told with puppets.` into **Brief (optional)**. Click **Start project**. You see **Project started.**
6. Click **People**. Click **Add a person**. Type `morgan@xr.test` into **Email address**. Type `Morgan Ray` into **Name (optional)**. Check **Director**. Click **Add person**. You see **Morgan Ray added as Director.**
7. Click **Add a person**. Type `riley@xr.test` into **Email address**. Type `Riley Chen` into **Name (optional)**. Check **Artist**. Click **Add person**. You see **Riley Chen added as Artist.**
8. Click **Add a person**. Type `sam@xr.test` into **Email address**. Type `Sam Okafor` into **Name (optional)**. Check **Technical artist**. Click **Add person**. You see **Sam Okafor added as Technical artist.**
9. Click **Add a person**. Type `drew@xr.test` into **Email address**. Type `Drew Patel` into **Name (optional)**. Check **Sound designer**. Click **Add person**. You see **Drew Patel added as Sound designer.** and **Invited, not signed in yet**.

## What you made

A passkey that signs you in on this device, and The Lantern Keeper, a project in Harbourfront Puppet Co., with its team of five: you as its owner, director and producer, and Morgan, Riley, Sam and Drew with their roles. Each of the four has an invitation email and has not signed in yet.

Next: Riley opens the invitation and signs in, in [Sign in and add a passkey](sign-in-and-add-a-passkey.md). Read [People and roles](../user/people/people-and-roles.md) for what each role gives.
