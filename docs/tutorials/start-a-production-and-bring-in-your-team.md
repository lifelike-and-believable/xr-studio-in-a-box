# Start a production and bring in your team

For **Jordan, the producer**.

You create your company's organisation, start a project in it, and add the four people who make it with you. Then you see the project as Riley, the artist, sees it.

## Before you start

- An XR Studio account that can start a project: on a new installation, the first person can.
- Jordan Lee is the producer. Jordan adds Morgan Ray, the director; Riley Chen, an artist; Sam Okafor, a technical artist; and Drew Patel, a sound designer.

## Steps

1. Sign in as **Jordan**. You see **Projects**.
2. Open `/organisations/new`. Type `Harbourfront Puppet Co.` into **Organisation name**. Click **Create organisation**. You see **Looked after by**.
3. Click **Start a project in this organisation**. Type `The Lantern Keeper` into **Project name**. Type `A lighthouse keeper's last night, told with puppets.` into **Brief (optional)**. Click **Start project**. You see **Project started.**
4. Click **People**. Click **Add a person**. Type `morgan@xr.test` into **Email address**. Type `Morgan Ray` into **Name (optional)**. Check **Director**. Click **Add person**. You see **Morgan Ray added as Director.**
5. Click **Add a person**. Type `riley@xr.test` into **Email address**. Type `Riley Chen` into **Name (optional)**. Check **Artist**. Click **Add person**. You see **Riley Chen added as Artist.**
6. Click **Add a person**. Type `sam@xr.test` into **Email address**. Type `Sam Okafor` into **Name (optional)**. Check **Technical artist**. Click **Add person**. You see **Sam Okafor added as Technical artist.**
7. Click **Add a person**. Type `drew@xr.test` into **Email address**. Type `Drew Patel` into **Name (optional)**. Check **Sound designer**. Click **Add person**. You see **Drew Patel added as Sound designer.** and **Invited, not signed in yet**.
8. As **Riley**: Sign in as **Riley**. Riley opens the invitation. You see **What needs you**.
9. As **Riley**: Click **Projects**. Click **The Lantern Keeper**. Click **People**. You see **Jordan Lee** and **Riley Chen**.

## What you made

The Lantern Keeper, a project in Harbourfront Puppet Co., with its team of five: you as its owner, director and producer, and Morgan, Riley, Sam and Drew with their roles. Riley has opened the invitation and can see the project but not change who is on it.
