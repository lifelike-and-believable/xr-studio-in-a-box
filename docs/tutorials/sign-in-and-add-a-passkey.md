# Sign in and add a passkey

For **Riley, the artist**.

Anyone who has been added to a project does the same. You open your invitation, sign out, sign in again with an email link, and add a passkey so that you can sign in without an email.

## Before you start

- Riley Chen is an artist on The Lantern Keeper. Jordan Lee, the producer, has added Riley, and Riley has an invitation email.
- A browser with a passkey provider: your device or a password manager.

## Steps

1. As **Riley**: Sign in as **Riley**. Riley opens the link in the invitation email. You see **What needs you**.
2. As **Riley**: Click **Riley Chen**. Click **Sign out**. You see **You are signed out**.
3. As **Riley**: Click **Sign in again**. Type `riley@xr.test` into **Email address**. Click **Email me a sign-in link**. You see **Check your email**.
4. As **Riley**: Open the link in the email "Your XR Studio sign-in link". You see **What needs you**. (manual)
5. As **Riley**: Select **Riley Chen**, then **Your account**. Under **Passkeys**, select **Add a passkey** and confirm in your browser or password manager. The passkey is listed with the device it was made on and today's date. (manual)
6. As **Riley**: Select **Riley Chen**, then **Sign out**. Select **Sign in again**, then **Sign in with a passkey**, and confirm. You see **What needs you**. (manual)

## What you made

Riley's account, signed in with the invitation, an email link and a passkey. Next: read [Signing in](../user/signing-in/sign-in.md) for what to do when a link no longer works.
